# Bloom Health privacy policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Bloom Health helps you build daily habits, log workouts, track your cycle and chat with Bloomy, an AI assistant. This policy explains what we collect, why, who it goes to and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- Account details: your name, email address, phone number, avatar, gender (optional) and time zone.
- Habits and progress: the habits you create, what you complete each day, streaks, your daily goal and reminder times.
- Workouts: the workouts you log and how long they took.
- Period tracker: the period dates you enter, if the tracker is on.
- Steps: if you turn on Auto-track steps, your daily step totals from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone). We read step counts only, nothing else.
- Bloomy chats: the messages you send to Bloomy and its replies.
- Technical data: sign-in tokens and basic request logs (time, address, errors) that keep the service secure and working.

## How we use it

- To run the app: save your habits and progress, sync them between your devices and show your streaks.
- To sign you in with a one-time code sent to your email.
- To fill your Steps habit from your step totals.
- To answer you in Bloomy.
- To keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data for advertising or to build profiles about you.

## Health data

Steps, workouts and period data are health information. We use them only to show your progress in the app. We never use them for advertising, never sell them and never share them with data brokers.

Bloom Health's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Apple Health is used the same way.

You can turn off Auto-track steps at any time in Settings, or remove the permission in Health Connect or the Health app.

## Bloomy and AI

When you message Bloomy, the conversation is sent to Groq, an AI provider, to write the reply. Only the chat itself and our fixed instructions are sent, not your name, email or health records.

Groq processes these messages under its own terms and privacy policy. Don't share anything in a chat you wouldn't want an AI service to process.

Bloomy's answers can be wrong and aren't medical advice. Talk to a doctor about health concerns.

## Who else sees your data

- People you share your space with (viewers) can see your habits, progress and steps. They never see your period data.
- Service providers who run Bloom Health for us: cloud hosting and databases (Vercel, MongoDB Atlas, Google Cloud), email delivery for sign-in codes, and Groq for Bloomy. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
- Authorities, if the law requires it.

## On your phone

The app keeps a copy of your data on your phone so it works offline, and stores your sign-in securely in the system keychain. Reminders, alarms and home-screen widgets run on your phone. Signing out clears your sign-in.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with access limited to the people who run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. Sign-in codes expire within minutes and old sign-in sessions expire on their own.

To delete your account and all its data, including habits, workouts, steps, period data and Bloomy chats, email support.bloomhealth@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also ask for a copy of your data, or for corrections, the same way.

## Children

Bloom Health is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

## Contact

Questions or requests: support.bloomhealth@gmail.com
