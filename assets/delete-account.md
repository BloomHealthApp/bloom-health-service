# Delete your Bloom Health account

_App: Bloom Health · Developer: BloomHealth · Last updated: 7 November 2026_

You can delete your Bloom Health account and all its data at any time.

## How to delete your account

- Step 1: Open the Bloom Health app and sign in.
- Step 2: Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
- Step 3: Read what will be erased, tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Can't use the app? Email support.bloomhealth@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll handle it the same way and confirm by email.

## What happens next

- Straight away: your account is locked and you're signed out on every device. Nobody can use it, and anyone you share your space with loses access.
- For 15 days: changed your mind? Sign in and tap Restore my account to keep everything as it was.
- After 15 days: your account and all its data are erased permanently from our servers. This can't be undone.

## What is deleted

- Your profile: name, email address, phone number, avatar, gender and time zone
- Your habits, daily progress and streaks
- Your workouts and step totals
- Your period tracker data
- Your Bloomy chats
- Your sign-in sessions and any pending sign-in codes

The copy of your data on your phone is erased when you sign out, which happens automatically when you delete your account.

## What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Our basic request logs (time, route and error codes, with no names, emails or health data) expire on their own. You can sign up again later with the same email, but your old data won't come back.
